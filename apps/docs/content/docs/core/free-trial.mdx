Dokploy Cloud offers a **7-day free trial** on the Hobby and Startup plans. No credit card is required to start it.

## Starting the Trial

1. Register on [Dokploy Cloud](https://app.dokploy.com).
2. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
3. Click **Start 7-day free trial** on the plan you want to try.

The trial starts immediately. During the trial you can connect:

| Plan | Servers during the trial |
| --- | --- |
| **Hobby** | 1 |
| **Startup** | 3 |

The limits of the plan you chose apply during the trial. See [Cloud vs Self-hosted](/docs/core/differences) for what each plan includes.

## During the Trial

The **Billing** page shows the days left and the date the trial ends. From there you can:

- **Switch plan** between Hobby and Startup without restarting the trial.
- **Switch between monthly and annual billing.**
- **Add a payment method**, so the subscription continues when the trial ends.

Dokploy emails you before the trial expires.

## When the Trial Ends

- **With a payment method on file**: the subscription starts and is billed automatically.
- **Without a payment method**: the trial subscription is cancelled and your servers are deactivated in Dokploy until you subscribe.

<Callout type="info">
  Deactivating a server in Dokploy does not touch the machine. Your containers keep running on your own server; you only lose the ability to manage them from Dokploy Cloud until the plan is active again.
</Callout>

## Limits

- One trial per account. Once a trial has been used, it cannot be started again.
- A trial cannot be started on an account that already has an active plan.
