**Self-hosted Restrictions** are two instance-wide switches for teams that need tighter control over how people sign in and where workloads run.

<Callout type="info">
  Both switches require a valid [Enterprise license](/docs/core/enterprise/license-keys) and are only available on **self-hosted** instances. They are not available on Dokploy Cloud.
</Callout>

You find them in **Settings → SSO**, in the **Self-hosted Restrictions** card.

## Enforce SSO

When enabled, the email/password login form is hidden and users must sign in exclusively through SSO.

The restriction is enforced on the server, not only in the UI. With Enforce SSO on, these sign-in methods are rejected:

- Email and password sign-in and sign-up.
- Social sign-in.
- Passkey sign-in.

<Callout type="warn">
  Enable Enforce SSO only after you have registered an [SSO provider](/docs/core/enterprise/sso) and confirmed that you can sign in with it. Otherwise nobody, including the owner, can sign in from the login page.
</Callout>

## Remote Servers Only

When enabled, all services — applications, databases and Docker Compose — must be deployed to a [remote server](/docs/core/remote-servers). Deploying directly to the machine that runs Dokploy is not allowed.

Use it to keep the Dokploy host as a pure control plane: builds and workloads run on other machines, so a heavy build or a misbehaving container cannot take the panel down.

Before enabling it, add at least one remote server. Creating a service without selecting a server fails while the option is on.
